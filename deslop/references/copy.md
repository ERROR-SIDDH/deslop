# Copy: from template voice to MNC voice

Premium copy is specific, calm and checkable. The live MNC heroes we studied:
- Stripe: "Financial infrastructure to grow your revenue."
- Linear: "The product development system for teams and agents."
- Apple: just the product name.

## Headline formula
**[Category noun] for/to [concrete outcome]**, or simply the product name plus one fact. Sentence case, no exclamation mark, no gradient word.

| Slop | Deslopped |
|---|---|
| Learning that **thinks** the way you do. | Spaced-repetition tutoring that adapts to what you forget. |
| Close More Deals. Practice Makes Perfect. | Sales roleplay with AI buyers, scored on every call. |
| Scale Your Business with AI Automation | Workflow automation for finance and ops teams. |
| Building Digital Products That Create Real Impact. | We design and build web and mobile products for early-stage teams. |
| Trade crypto with confidence & security | Crypto trading with institutional custody and real-time risk limits. |

The deslopped lines are illustrations. Build the real line from facts in the project (README, existing copy, product data). When a fact is missing, ask for it or keep the claim general. Never invent one.

## Subhead
One sentence of about 20 words or fewer, with 2–3 concrete verbs and objects. "Accept payments, send payouts and manage revenue in one integration." Not "Unlock the power of seamless, AI-driven growth."

## Word swaps

| Cut | Use instead |
|---|---|
| seamless(ly) | delete, or name the integration ("syncs with Salesforce") |
| unlock / unleash / supercharge / elevate | the actual verb: send, track, approve, deploy, reconcile |
| empower | let, help, or delete |
| revolutionize / transform / game-changing | say what is measurably different |
| cutting-edge / next-gen / state-of-the-art | delete |
| effortless / in seconds / in minutes | a real number you can defend, or delete |
| all-in-one platform | list the 2–3 things it actually does |
| AI-powered X | the outcome; mention AI in the body if relevant |
| Get Started → / Start Free Trial → | "Start for free" / "Contact sales" / "Book a demo" (no arrow glyph needed) |
| Watch Demo | "See how it works" as a text link |

## Hygiene
- No emoji in headings, buttons, list bullets or stats.
- Sentence case everywhere: headlines, buttons, nav.
- Section labels are plain nouns ("Pricing", "Security", "Integrations"), not "Why Choose Us?" or "How It Works ✨".
- Stats only if they are real and sourced. "Trusted by 2,000+ teams" with no logos becomes a single named customer story, or nothing.
- Testimonials: real name, role and company, with no star rows. If they are placeholders, flag them to the user.
- CTA microcopy like "No credit card required · Free forever" is fine *once*, in muted text, without checkmark icons.
